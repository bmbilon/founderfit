# FounderFit v2.1 — Thesis Integrity
## Question 1: High-Stakes Anomaly Response

### Placement
- Section: Thesis Integrity
- Order: Final section of assessment
- Format: Multiple choice (single-select)
- Fatigue leverage: intentional

---

### Prompt

You're involved in developing something with serious real-world impact — for example, a flying vehicle or a life-saving medical treatment.

Early tests support your explanation for why it works. As trials expand, a growing number of edge cases appear. None are disastrous, but they don't fully fit your original understanding.

Momentum matters, but being wrong would have meaningful consequences.

What do you do next?

---

### Options

A. Reframe how the edge cases are interpreted. Early breakthroughs often produce messy data, and the core explanation still holds.

B. Continue testing without changing the explanation yet. It's too early to revise until the signal is clearer.

C. Adjust the explanation to account for the edge cases, even if it becomes less elegant or cohesive.

D. Actively explore alternative explanations that could better account for both the original results and the anomalies.

---

## Scoring Model (Internal)

This question contributes to three latent signals:
- Epistemic Humility
- Narrative Protection vs Truth Seeking
- Action Bias Under Uncertainty

### Base Thesis Integrity Impact (pre-demographics)

| Option | Score Impact | Notes |
|------|-------------|------|
| A | −18 | Narrative protection, ego defense |
| B | +4 | Passive caution |
| C | +12 | Adaptive revision |
| D | +22 | Strong falsification mindset |

Option A triggers a **hubris-adjacent risk marker** (not a fail on its own).

---

## Demographic Modulation Rules

### Age
- <30: C/D amplified
- 45–55: A penalized harder, D capped
- 55+: Excessive D down-weighted

### Industry Experience
- <3 years: B slightly boosted
- 3–10 years: C/D maximized
- 10+ years: A strongly penalized

### Prior Successful Exit
- None: D rewarded fully
- Prior exit: D −15%
- Multiple exits: A escalates to red flag

### Team Structure
- Solo: Repeated D dampened
- 2–3 founders: D fully rewarded
- 4+ founders: C often optimal

---

## Anti-Gaming Rule
If Option D is selected frequently AND vanity / dominance signals are high elsewhere,
D is reclassified as performative humility and down-weighted.
